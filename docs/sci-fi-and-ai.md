# Sci-Fi and AI

For robots, digital worlds, game-era graphics, AI visuals, and science-fiction transformations.

## When This Group Fits

Use it when the subject should move into a technological, game-like, robotic, or virtual world.

## Examples

- `/bossfight + /retroarcade`: Create a retro arcade boss-fight scene where a giant coffee machine is the final boss. Use pixel-game energy, dramatic UI-style health bars, glowing attacks made of steam, and a dark arcade color palette. Keep the scene playful and readable.
- `/robot + /datastream`: Turn a customer-support mascot into a helpful robot surrounded by clean data streams. Keep the expression friendly, not threatening.
- `/ps1 + /alienworld`: Create a low-poly PS1-era alien-world exploration scene with a tiny rover and simple readable shapes.
