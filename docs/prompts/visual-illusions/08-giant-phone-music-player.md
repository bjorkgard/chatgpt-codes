# Giant Phone Music Player

![Giant phone music player example](../../../examples/prompts/visual-illusions/08-giant-phone-music-player.png)

## Prompt

```text
Create a photorealistic cinematic street scene featuring a giant modern smartphone lying diagonally across a city sidewalk. A stylish woman sits naturally on the oversized phone, wearing a fitted brown long-sleeve top, high-waisted cream trousers, and white sneakers. Preserve her facial features and realistic proportions.

The phone screen displays a premium music-player interface with her portrait, the title "THIS IS ME," her name "Ingrid Lara," playback controls, progress bar, and playlist details.

Include realistic reflections, glass highlights, metallic red phone edges, pedestrians and urban street elements in the background, warm natural daylight, dramatic perspective, high-angle composition, ultra-detailed professional photography, realistic shadows, depth of field, 4K, vertical portrait.
```

## Usage Notes

For better generated results, keep the music-player interface simple. Ask for one large portrait, one title, one artist name, simple controls, and a progress bar instead of many playlist details.
