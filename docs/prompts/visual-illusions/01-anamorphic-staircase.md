# Anamorphic Staircase

![Anamorphic staircase example](../../../examples/prompts/visual-illusions/01-anamorphic-staircase.png)

## Prompt

```text
Hyperrealistic photograph of a 3D anamorphic painting on an old stone staircase.

Transform the person into a photorealistic 3D anamorphic pavement painting on a stone staircase. The artwork must be geometrically distorted so that, from the camera's viewpoint, it appears as a real, three-dimensional person lying on the steps. Preserve realistic painted shadows, integrate the artwork into each step and riser, maintain perfect perspective alignment, and create a convincing trompe-l'oeil optical illusion: ultrarealistic street art.

The painting depicts a person lying on their side, resting on the steps. The illustration is fully integrated with each step through precise anamorphic distortion.

Viewed from the correct vantage point, the figure appears completely three-dimensional and real, creating a perfect optical illusion.

The hand in the foreground is very close to the lens and appears slightly oversized due to perspective. The head rests on the bent arm.

Extremely realistic textures of skin, clothing, aged stone, cracks, dirt, and moss.

Painted shadows perfectly aligned with natural lighting.

Historic stone staircase with side walls and lush vegetation in the background.

Forced perspective, trompe-l'oeil, street art, anamorphic illusion, pavement art, optical illusion, 3D anamorphic street art, anamorphic chalk art, perspective illusion, sidewalk painting, photorealistic street painting, single viewpoint illusion.

Photograph taken from the single point where the illusion works.

Ultra photorealistic, 8K, HDR, global illumination, ray tracing, physically based rendering (PBR), cinematic composition, Canon EOS R5, RF 24-70mm f/2.8L, f/5.6, ISO 100, maximum detail, natural colors, original aspect ratio.
```

## Usage Notes

Use this when you want a single-viewpoint optical illusion rather than a normal person on stairs. The key instruction is that the artwork must be visibly integrated into the stair treads and risers while appearing three-dimensional from one camera position.
