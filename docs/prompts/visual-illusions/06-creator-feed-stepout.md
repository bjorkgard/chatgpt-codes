# Creator Feed Step-Out

![Creator feed step-out example](../../../examples/prompts/visual-illusions/06-creator-feed-stepout.png)

## Prompt

```text
Use IMAGE 1 as the strict primary reference for the woman's identity, face, facial proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, body type and overall appearance. Preserve her recognizable features naturally and realistically. Use IMAGE 2 as the strict reference for the social-media feed, profile layout, visual hierarchy, colors, typography placement, post grid and overall interface aesthetic.

Create a premium photorealistic vertical 4:5 advertising scene: a gigantic modern black smartphone standing inside a warm minimalist creator studio, showing the feed inspired by IMAGE 2. The woman from IMAGE 1 is physically stepping out from the phone screen toward the viewer, seamlessly transitioning from digital content into the real environment.

Keep her face faithful to IMAGE 1, with realistic pores, subtle natural skin texture, fine facial details, lifelike eyes, authentic hair strands, no plastic skin, no beauty-filter effect. Preserve her real expression and feminine presence. Outfit can follow the elegant neutral styling of the composition: fitted brown top, beige knit cardigan, ivory wide-leg trousers, white sneakers, subtle gold accessories, takeaway coffee and slim laptop.

All visible interface text must be in PORTUGUESE. Use labels such as: "psicologia", "fotos com IA", "reels", "faceless", "crescimento", "feminilidade", with clear readable typography.

Warm golden-hour light, beige studio, soft window shadows, realistic phone-glass reflections, cinematic depth, foreground camera, coffee, books, laptop and creator props. Sony A1, 50mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field, HDR, ultra-detailed 8K, editorial luxury aesthetic, realistic skin, fabric, hair and glass. No facial changes, no distorted anatomy, no extra fingers, no gibberish text.
```

## Usage Notes

This prompt expects two references: one for the person and one for the social feed layout. For standalone use, replace `IMAGE 1` and `IMAGE 2` with clear descriptions of the person and interface.
