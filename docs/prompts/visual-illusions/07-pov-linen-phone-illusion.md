# POV Linen Phone Illusion

![POV linen phone illusion example](../../../examples/prompts/visual-illusions/07-pov-linen-phone-illusion.png)

## Prompt

```text
Creative first-person POV fashion photograph, sophisticated European lifestyle aesthetic, surreal smartphone perspective illusion.

The photograph is captured entirely from the first-person point of view of a person looking straight down at their own body while seated. Their torso, thighs, knees and part of the shoes naturally occupy the background and edges of the composition, establishing a believable POV perspective.

The person wears an elegant European summer outfit made entirely from premium linen: a relaxed ivory linen shirt with an open collar, subtle natural wrinkles and sophisticated tailoring, paired with high-waisted warm beige or taupe linen trousers with a relaxed tailored fit and beautifully natural fabric draping. Refined minimalist leather loafers in warm brown or clean understated luxury footwear, subtle premium wristwatch and discreet accessories. Quiet luxury, Italian summer lifestyle, effortless European Riviera elegance, no visible logos.

In the center foreground, one hand holds a modern black smartphone vertically directly above the person's torso and lap. The hand enters naturally from the lower-left portion of the composition, wrist visible with a refined minimalist watch. The smartphone is large and sharply defined, occupying approximately one third of the central frame.

The smartphone screen becomes the main visual focal point and behaves like a surreal photographic window into another perspective of the same person.

Inside the smartphone screen, the same person is shown full-body sitting casually on an elegant stone step or low Mediterranean wall, photographed from a slightly elevated angle. Relaxed editorial pose: one knee raised toward the chest, the other leg bent naturally downward, forearms resting casually across the raised knee, hands relaxed, body slightly turned rather than perfectly frontal.

The outfit visible inside the phone must exactly match the real-world outfit: premium ivory linen shirt, warm beige tailored linen trousers, sophisticated brown leather loafers, minimalist watch and subtle accessories. Perfect wardrobe continuity between the person holding the smartphone and the person displayed inside the screen.

Create a clever impossible-perspective illusion: the person's legs and shoes displayed inside the smartphone subtly extend beyond the lower boundary of the phone screen and overlap the real hand holding the device, making it appear as though the miniature seated person is physically emerging from the smartphone.

The transition between digital screen and physical reality must be extremely clean and visually convincing. The legs emerging from the screen retain correct anatomy, scale, lighting and perspective. The smartphone remains physically visible behind them. No glowing portal, no science-fiction effects: the illusion should feel like sophisticated practical forced-perspective photography and seamless editorial compositing.

Outside the smartphone, the viewer sees the real person's linen trousers extending toward the upper left and upper right corners, creating two large diagonal shapes framing the central phone. One shoe is subtly visible near the upper edge. The real ivory linen shirt occupies much of the lower background behind the phone, creating a layered composition of linen textures, hand, smartphone and miniature subject.

Replace the ordinary asphalt environment with an elegant European setting: textured warm limestone pavement of a Mediterranean terrace, subtle natural imperfections in the stone, understated luxury environment, hints of European architecture and soft greenery appearing only around the outer edges of the composition. The environment remains secondary because the POV body and smartphone dominate the frame.

Strong visual hierarchy: real body as the background layer, hand and smartphone as the foreground layer, seated person inside the phone as the central focal point, legs emerging beyond the phone screen as the surreal connecting element.

Top-down POV composition, smartphone vertically centered, strong symmetry interrupted naturally by the hand, thighs framing both sides of the device, realistic wide-angle perspective, natural dimensional layering, believable scale and human relationships.

Soft Mediterranean daylight, warm late-afternoon sun, natural directional illumination, gentle realistic shadows, warm highlights across the linen fibers, subtle reflections on the smartphone glass and watch, elegant neutral color palette of ivory, cream, sand, taupe, warm limestone and muted brown.

Quiet luxury European summer fashion, Italian Riviera fashion editorial, contemporary luxury travel photography, sophisticated minimalism, understated elegance, natural candid feeling.

Shot with a Leica-style full-frame camera, f/4, realistic first-person wide-angle perspective, deep enough depth of field to preserve detail on the smartphone and surrounding clothing, subtle optical falloff toward the edges, realistic smartphone glass reflections, highly detailed linen fibers, authentic fabric folds, natural skin texture, soft highlight roll-off, premium editorial color grading, subtle film grain, cinematic photorealism.
```

## Usage Notes

This is a continuity-heavy prompt. The real outfit and the outfit inside the phone must match, and the emerging legs must overlap the phone edge without looking like a portal effect.
