# Surreal Scroll Stopper

![Surreal scroll stopper example](../../../examples/prompts/everyday-chatgpt-images/06-surreal-scroll-stopper.png)

## Prompt

```text
/wideframe + /portal + /dreamlogic + /goldenhour
Create a believable surreal social image of [person or object] moving between [ordinary place] and [unexpected place]. Use a visible portal or transition, consistent shadows, realistic scale, warm golden-hour light, and a wide frame that clearly shows both worlds. Make the impossible element feel physically integrated, not cartoonish. Vertical social format. No text, logos, distorted anatomy, or messy background details.
```

## Usage Notes

The illusion improves when the ordinary place, impossible destination, viewpoint, and lighting continuity are all named. Keep the transition physically believable instead of relying on a generic glowing portal.
