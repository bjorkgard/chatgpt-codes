# Cinematic Film Still

![Cinematic Film Still example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/019-reimagined-photo-cinematic-film-still.png)

## Prompt

```text
transform the uploaded image into a hyper-realistic cinematic film still, using the reference primarily as inspiration for
the subject, composition, and overall mood rather than something to recreate exactly. Lean heavily into realistic
filmmaking while freely reinterpreting facial features, proportions, clothing, hairstyle, lighting, environment, and
camera angle, keeping the subject generally recognizable. Prioritize creating a believable film scene over preserving
every detail of the original photograph.

Render the subject with natural anatomy, authentic skin texture, subtle pores, realistic hair strands, expressive eyes,
and lifelike facial expressions. Clothing should feature believable fabrics, natural folds, stitching, wear, and realistic
interaction with light.

Place the subject within a richly detailed cinematic environment that enhances the story. Reimagine backgrounds with
atmospheric depth, realistic architecture, environmental storytelling, and natural elements such as rain, fog, smoke,
dust, reflections, drifting particles, or soft haze.

Light the scene using motivated light sources, soft key lighting, gentle rim lighting, realistic shadows, subtle
volumetric light, rich contrast, natural depth of field, cinematic lens characteristics, delicate bokeh, and refined color
grading.

Compose the image with immersive framing, layered foreground, midground, and background depth, creating a
photorealistic, emotionally cinematic frame that feels captured from a high-budget feature film rather than an edited
photograph.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
