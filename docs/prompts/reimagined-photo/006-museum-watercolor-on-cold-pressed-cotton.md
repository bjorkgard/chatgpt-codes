# Museum Watercolor on Cold-pressed Cotton

![Museum Watercolor on Cold-pressed Cotton example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/006-reimagined-photo-museum-watercolor-on-cold-pressed-cotton.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear reference photo and use it as both the identity and composition reference. Faithfully preserve the
subject’s recognizable facial features, face shape, eye shape, smile, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, body proportions,
pose, clothing, accessories, camera angle, framing, lighting direction, environment, and emotional atmosphere so the
finished artwork is unmistakably the same moment.

Transform the entire image into an authentic, museum-quality watercolor painted by hand on premium cold-pressed
cotton paper. It should immediately read as a traditional watercolor—not a photo with a filter or a digital painting.

Reinterpret every element through watercolor using luminous transparent washes, layered glazing, expressive
wet-on-wet blending, soft pigment diffusion, natural blooms, granulation, backruns, uneven pigment settling, and
visible paper texture. Suggest detail with confident, economical brushwork rather than photographic precision. Let
secondary areas dissolve into soft washes, broken edges, atmospheric color, and intentional negative space while
giving the face, hands, and focal areas slightly more refinement without losing the watercolor character.

Preserve the original clothing, pose, composition, background, and lighting exactly, translating them into expressive
watercolor instead of redesigning them. Use a restrained harmonious palette, painterly simplicity, and handcrafted
imperfections. Avoid photorealism, hyper-detail, digital sharpening, acrylic or oil textures, opaque paint, cel shading,
heavy outlines, oversaturation, vector edges, or any appearance of a digital watercolor filter.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
