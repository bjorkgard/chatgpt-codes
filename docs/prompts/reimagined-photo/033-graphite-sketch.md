# Graphite Sketch

![Graphite Sketch example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/033-reimagined-photo-graphite-sketch.png)

## Prompt

```text
Transform it into a loose, expressive observational sketch, using the reference as inspiration for the subject, pose,
expression, body language, and composition rather than recreating it exactly. Freely reinterpret clothing, hairstyle,
colors, lighting, background, framing, and accessories while prioritizing expressive draftsmanship over photographic
realism.

Draw the scene as though an experienced artist captured it rapidly from life in a sketchbook. Emphasize confident
observation, movement, and personality with energetic graphite and charcoal-style marks.

Construct the image with bold contour lines, light exploratory strokes, visible construction lines, overlapping
corrections, and unfinished areas that reveal the creative process. Define forms with multiple searching lines instead
of perfect outlines, simplifying anatomy, clothing, hands, and objects into clear, economical shapes.

Vary line weight throughout the drawing, combining dark contours with faint guidelines, broken lines, quick
cross-contours, and scribbled accents. Build depth using loose hatching, cross-hatching, tonal scribbling, and soft
graphite shading with visible pencil texture rather than smooth gradients.

Leave generous areas unfinished, allowing backgrounds and secondary elements to dissolve into sketch marks or
blank paper. Add restrained perspective guides, proportion lines, erased marks, and shape-blocking geometry.
Render on warm ivory, lightly textured drawing paper with visible tooth, preserving generous negative space for an
authentic studio sketch.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.
