# Oil Pastel Fashion Illustration

![Oil Pastel Fashion Illustration example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/038-reimagined-photo-oil-pastel-fashion-illustration.png)

## Prompt

```text
Transform it into a sophisticated editorial fashion illustration rendered with richly layered oil pastel and wax-crayon
textures. Preserve the subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, pose, clothing, proportions, hairstyle,
accessories, camera angle, and overall composition.

Simplify the surrounding environment into loose, impressionistic shapes, retaining only essential architectural forms
and major color relationships. Let background details dissolve into expressive pastel marks, unfinished areas, and
generous negative space so the figure remains the focal point.

Construct the subject with broad masses of color and simplified painterly forms rather than precise outlines. Use
thick, tactile pastel strokes with visible pressure, direction, overlapping pigment, broken color, and uneven buildup.
Blend selectively while preserving authentic handmade texture. Avoid smooth gradients, polished digital rendering, or
photographic realism.

Keep facial features recognizable but simplified with economical marks. Emphasize clothing through bold color
blocks, soft folds, layered strokes, and expressive texture.

Render on subtly textured off-white art paper with visible grain showing through selected areas. Reserve the
strongest color, contrast, and detail for the subject and outfit.

The finished illustration should feel contemporary, refined, airy, expressive, handcrafted,
and confidently unfinished.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.
