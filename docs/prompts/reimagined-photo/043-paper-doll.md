# Paper Doll

![Paper Doll example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/043-reimagined-photo-paper-doll.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear full-body reference photo. Preserve the original composition, pose, facial features, hairstyle, clothing,
expression, proportions, lighting, and background as faithfully as possible. Rather than reimagining the subject,
transform the existing photograph into a handcrafted paper doll presentation by layering subtle paper craft elements
on top of the original image. The final result should look like the original photo has been carefully cut out and turned
into a collectible paper doll while maintaining the authenticity of the photograph.

Create a clean white cut outline that closely follows the silhouette of the subject, leaving a consistent narrow border
around the entire figure. Add small folded paper attachment tabs extending from the edges of the cutout around the
arms, shoulders, waist, legs, and other natural locations, giving the impression that the figure has been assembled
from folded paper. The tabs should be simple white paper with soft shadows and gentle folds, appearing neatly
attached without obscuring important details.

Place the cutout on top of the original background so it appears slightly raised from the photograph. Add subtle paper
thickness, delicate edge shadows, and a faint drop shadow beneath the cutout to reinforce the layered handcrafted
effect while keeping the illusion elegant and believable. Preserve every detail of the clothing, textures, colors, and
lighting inside the cutout exactly as they appear in the reference photo.

The overall aesthetic should feel like a premium paper craft mockup or modern scrapbook design, with realistic paper
textures, subtle dimensionality, and meticulous attention to detail. The transformation should celebrate the original
photograph rather than replace it, making it instantly recognizable while adding the handcrafted paper doll illusion.

VARIATION:

Now create this version: the photographed woman becomes the base paper doll, while her blouse, jeans, shoes,
sunglasses, jewelry, and possibly tote are presented as separate paper clothing/accessory pieces beside her, each
with small white folding tabs.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
