# Film Noir Sketch

![Film Noir Sketch example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/014-reimagined-photo-film-noir-sketch.png)

## Prompt

```text
Transform the uploaded image into a dramatic film noir black-and-white sketch illustration, using the reference
primarily as inspiration for the subject, composition, and overall scene rather than something to recreate exactly. Lean
heavily into the artistic style, allowing the illustration to reinterpret facial features, clothing, lighting, proportions, and
background while keeping the subject generally recognizable. The finished image should immediately read as a
hand-crafted noir illustration rather than a stylized photograph.

Render the artwork entirely in black, white, and subtle charcoal-gray tones, eliminating all color. Use bold expressive
ink linework combined with loose graphite and charcoal sketch textures, dramatic cross-hatching, dense hatching,
stippling, rough pencil marks, and layered shading to create depth. Emphasize strong chiaroscuro lighting with deep
blacks, bright highlights, and heavy cinematic shadows that obscure portions of the face and environment.
Incorporate visible paper texture, imperfect hand-drawn lines, smudged graphite, dry-brush ink effects, and subtle film
grain to create an authentic vintage detective illustration.

Reimagine the scene with a classic 1940s noir atmosphere, featuring rain-slick streets, glowing street lamps, drifting
cigarette smoke, fog, reflective pavement, vintage architecture, old automobiles, neon hotel signs, alleyways, fire
escapes, dramatic backlighting, or other timeless noir elements whenever appropriate. Feel free to completely
redesign the background to better support the cinematic mood rather than preserving the original setting.

Compose the image with strong visual storytelling using dynamic camera angles, asymmetrical framing, dramatic
silhouettes, and cinematic perspective. Favor close-up portraits, over-the-shoulder compositions, mysterious side
profiles, or wide environmental shots that evoke suspense and tension. Lighting should feel motivated by a single
hard light source—streetlights, neon signs, moonlight, or headlights—creating bold contrast and long dramatic
shadows.

The overall aesthetic should resemble a masterfully hand-illustrated detective graphic novel, blending vintage ink
illustration, charcoal sketching, and classic noir cinema into a timeless, moody, high-contrast black-and-white artwork
that prioritizes atmosphere and artistic interpretation over photographic accuracy.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
