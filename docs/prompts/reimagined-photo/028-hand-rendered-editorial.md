# Hand-Rendered Editorial

![Hand-Rendered Editorial example](../../../examples/prompts/reimagined-photo/028-reimagined-photo-hand-rendered-editorial.png)

## Prompt

```text
Transform it into an elegant hand-rendered editorial illustration, using the original
primarily for the subject, pose, expression, composition, and mood rather than
recreating it exactly. Freely reinterpret clothing, hairstyle, colors, lighting, background,
and framing while preserving the subject's personality. Prioritize expressive artistry over
photographic accuracy.

Illustrate the scene with watercolor, ink, graphite, and colored pencil. Replace realism
with confident linework, loose sketching, translucent watercolor washes, layered pencil
shading, and expressive ink accents. Simplify forms into graceful shapes while
maintaining believable proportions.

Reveal the artistic process with a few subtle construction marks, such as faint facial
guidelines, light perspective lines, partially erased sketch strokes, or simple alignment
marks. Keep them delicate, authentic, and unobtrusive.

Use varied ink line weights, broken contours, crosshatching, dry-brush textures, and
unfinished edges that naturally blend into watercolor washes. Paint on textured
watercolor paper with visible grain, soft blooms, pigment pooling, feathered edges, and
transparent glazing.

Choose a refined palette of natural skin tones, warm neutrals, earthy browns, terracotta,
muted greens, gentle blues, warm grays, and restrained accent colors. Simplify
backgrounds into loose architectural or landscape shapes with fading washes and
visible paper.

Add Pencil Sketch constructions lines and guidelines, especially on the face

The final artwork should feel handcrafted, expressive, timeless, and worthy of a
beautifully illustrated editorial feature.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these when you have a reference photo and want the same subject reinterpreted in a new artistic style. Keep identity, pose, and composition instructions specific when those details matter.
