# Travel Poster

![Travel Poster example](../../../examples/prompts/poster-prompts/062-poster-prompts-travel-poster.png)

## Prompt

```text
Create a nostalgic mid-century vintage travel poster celebrating [PLACE]. Design the artwork as a beautifully
illustrated retro tourism advertisement inspired by classic European travel posters from the 1920s through the 1960s.
Prioritize bold graphic composition, timeless elegance, and simplified painterly realism over photographic detail.

Compose the scene around an iconic landmark, landscape, city, or destination that instantly represents [SUBJECT].
Simplify architecture, trees, mountains, water, vehicles, and environmental elements into clean, stylized shapes with
confident silhouettes and balanced compositions. Eliminate unnecessary detail while maintaining recognizable forms
and a sense of grandeur.

Render the illustration with hand-painted gouache and vintage lithograph aesthetics featuring soft brushwork, subtle
texture, gentle gradients, flat graphic shapes, and lightly weathered print imperfections. Avoid photorealism, CGI, or
modern digital illustration techniques.

Use a warm, slightly faded color palette inspired by vintage printing, including muted terracotta, olive green, golden
ochre, dusty blue, warm cream, burnt sienna, faded teal, and soft sepia. Introduce subtle paper aging, gentle grain,
and lightly worn edges to evoke an authentic printed poster.

Feature elegant period-inspired typography integrated naturally into the composition with large destination names,
classic serif lettering, optional handwritten script accents, and tasteful travel slogans. Keep typography clean,
balanced, and reminiscent of historic tourism advertisements.

Frame the artwork with generous negative space, bold visual hierarchy, and a premium poster layout that feels
suitable for framing. The finished image should immediately read as an authentic vintage travel advertisement
discovered in a historic railway station or grand hotel rather than a modern illustration.
```

## Usage Notes

Replace bracketed inputs such as season, month, destination, activity count, or zodiac sign before generating.
