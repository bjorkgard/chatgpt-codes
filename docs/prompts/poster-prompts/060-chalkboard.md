# Chalkboard

![Chalkboard example](../../../examples/prompts/poster-prompts/060-poster-prompts-chalkboard.png)

## Prompt

```text
Create a whimsical hand-drawn chalkboard illustration of [subject]. Render the entire scene on a rich
matte black chalkboard using authentic chalk textures, colorful chalk strokes, powdery smudges, erased
areas, sketch lines, and visible chalk dust. Illustrate everything with rough, imperfect hand-drawn chalk
marks rather than smooth digital lines. Simplify forms into playful classroom-style doodles with expressive
outlines, exaggerated proportions, and childlike charm. Surround the main subject with colorful chalk
illustrations such as stars, arrows, flowers, clouds, rainbows, banners, handwritten notes, books, swirls,
sparkles, and decorative borders that fill the composition naturally. Use vibrant classroom chalk colors
including white, blue, green, yellow, orange, pink, purple, and red. Incorporate playful hand-lettered
typography wherever appropriate using imperfect chalk lettering. The finished artwork should feel
nostalgic, educational, whimsical, and unmistakably hand-drawn, with visible chalk texture, dusty
blending, erased marks, and an authentic classroom chalkboard aesthetic rather than a polished digital
illustration.
```

## Usage Notes

Replace bracketed inputs such as season, month, destination, activity count, or zodiac sign before generating.
