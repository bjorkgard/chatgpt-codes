# Zodiac Poster

![Zodiac Poster example](../../../examples/prompts/poster-prompts/063-poster-prompts-zodiac-poster.png)

## Prompt

```text
Create a premium modern minimalist wall art print inspired by the selected Zodiac Sign. [INSERT ZODIAC SIGN]. The finished
design should feel like a professionally designed piece of contemporary home décor—something you would proudly frame and
display in a stylish living room, bedroom, office, or gallery wall.

The composition should embrace clean design principles with generous negative space, balanced visual hierarchy, and a refined
editorial aesthetic. Every element should feel intentional and sophisticated rather than decorative or cluttered.

Research and accurately represent the selected zodiac sign by incorporating its authentic symbolism, personality traits, ruling
element, traditional glyph, associated constellation, and other recognizable astrological characteristics. The artwork should
immediately evoke the sign without feeling like an infographic.

Choose a thoughtfully curated color palette that reflects the sign’s personality. Use muted, earthy, neutral, jewel-toned, or softly
saturated colors where appropriate rather than overly bright or neon hues. Colors should feel cohesive, elevated, and suitable for
modern interior design.

Include a tasteful selection of visual elements inspired by the zodiac sign, such as celestial motifs, abstract geometric forms,
botanical accents, organic linework, subtle constellations, moons, stars, suns, or symbolic illustrations. Keep every graphic simple
and elegant—avoid excessive ornamentation or busy compositions.

Feature the zodiac sign’s name as the primary text element using refined typography. You may also include the zodiac glyph, the
ruling element (Fire, Earth, Air, or Water), and 3–6 carefully chosen descriptive words or short phrases that capture the essence of
the sign. Examples include qualities like “Curious,” “Grounded,” “Visionary,” or “Loyal.” Keep typography minimal and beautifully
spaced.

Optionally include one short, tasteful sentence (no more than 12 words) that captures the spirit of the sign. Avoid long descriptions,
paragraphs, bullet lists, or horoscope-style text.

Allow the visual design—not the wording—to communicate most of the personality. The artwork should feel calm, timeless, and
intentional.

The overall composition should resemble a high-end Scandinavian, Japanese, or boutique editorial art print with clean typography,
restrained graphics, elegant spacing, and museum-quality design. Avoid gradients, glossy effects, 3D rendering, clip art, cartoon
styling, ornate borders, decorative flourishes, excessive icons, or dense layouts.

The finished artwork should look like a premium designer print sold by a luxury home décor brand: modern, balanced, sophisticated,
and instantly frame-worthy.
```
