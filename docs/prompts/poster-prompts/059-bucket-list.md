# Bucket List

![Bucket List example](../../../examples/prompts/poster-prompts/059-poster-prompts-bucket-list.png)

## Prompt

```text
Create a charming illustrated “[Season/Month] Bucket List” poster in a cute modern preppy stationery aesthetic with
a clean, minimalist doodle style inspired by trendy planner stickers, boutique greeting cards, and printable wall art.
Use a vertical 4:5 layout with a warm cream or soft off-white background, a large playful bubble-letter title at the top in
bright pink with a subtle aqua drop shadow, and a balanced grid of [9] simple illustrated activities beneath it. Generate
activities that naturally fit [Season/Month], local weather, holidays, traditions, seasonal foods, festivals, nature, and
school schedules when appropriate. Each activity should consist of one adorable, highly simplified flat vector-style
doodle icon with thick black outlines, rounded shapes, cheerful pastel colors, minimal shading, and a short
handwritten-style caption underneath (2–5 words). Scatter a few tiny decorative filler doodles throughout the
page—such as hearts, stars, bows, sparkles, dots, moons, bats, leaves, flowers, smiley faces, or seasonal icons—to
create a playful composition without clutter. Keep plenty of white space, consistent icon sizing, crisp edges, and a
cohesive color palette so every element feels like a collectible sticker sheet. The overall aesthetic should be cute,
polished, modern, share-worthy, family-friendly, and instantly recognizable as a premium printable bucket list,
avoiding realistic rendering, detailed textures, gradients, heavy shadows, or overly complex illustrations.
```

## Usage Notes

Replace bracketed inputs such as season, month, destination, activity count, or zodiac sign before generating.
