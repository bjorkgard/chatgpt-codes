# Sydney

![Sydney travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/03-sydney.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SYDNEY

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for Sydney.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep Sydney 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "Sydney" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: the Opera House with overlapping white shell forms, and the Harbour Bridge as a large steel arch spanning the water.
Secondary elements: deep harbour water, small sailing boats, downtown tower clusters, eucalyptus foliage, a low headland, sunlit waterfront edges.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the landmarks into generic circles, rectangles, or decorative symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "SYDNEY" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the Opera House and bridge forming the main silhouette.

Geometric language: use shell triangles, bridge arcs, flat harbour bands, simplified sails, headland curves, tower blocks, and foliage circles. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmark.

Palette: bright southern daylight with clear sky blue, deep harbour blue, chalk white, soft sand, muted eucalyptus green, faint coral, warm grey, and off-white. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "SYDNEY", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the Opera House shells or bridge arch, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

The shell shapes should stay crisp and layered; the bridge can be simplified, but its arch must remain obvious.
