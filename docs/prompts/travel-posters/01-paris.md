# Paris

![Paris travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/01-paris.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - PARIS

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for Paris.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep Paris 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "Paris" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: the Eiffel Tower with its iron lattice tapering to a narrow point, and Sacre-Coeur as a white domed basilica sitting on a hill.
Secondary elements: grey zinc Haussmann rooftops with mansard slopes, chimney stacks, the Seine as a soft horizontal band, low stone arch bridges, plane trees, wrought-iron balcony rhythms.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the landmarks into generic circles, rectangles, or decorative symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "PARIS" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the Eiffel Tower as the first focal point and Sacre-Coeur rising behind the rooftops.

Geometric language: use arches, rooftop triangles, soft river bands, stepped building blocks, tree circles, and muted color fields to organize the scene. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmark.

Palette: cool Parisian daylight with pale sky blue, soft grey-lilac, zinc grey, warm cream stone, muted rose, faded olive, charcoal, and off-white. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "PARIS", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the Eiffel Tower, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

Keep the Eiffel Tower large enough to anchor the poster; Sacre-Coeur and the roofline should read as Parisian support rather than competing focal points.
