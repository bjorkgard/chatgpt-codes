# Rome

![Rome travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/14-rome.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - ROME

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for Rome.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep Rome 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "Rome" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: the Colosseum with stacked tiers of arched openings, and the dome of St Peter's Basilica rising behind the city.
Secondary elements: umbrella pines with flat spreading canopies, ochre and terracotta facades, ancient columns, the Tiber as a muted river band, low stone bridges, warm masonry.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the Colosseum arches or basilica dome into generic symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "ROME" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the Colosseum as the main foreground landmark and St Peter's dome in the distance.

Geometric language: use arch rows, dome curves, stone bands, pine canopy disks, column verticals, bridge arcs, and layered warm facades. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmarks.

Palette: golden Roman stone with warm ochre, faded terracotta, dusty sienna, pale cream, deep pine green, soft sky blue, limestone grey, and off-white. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "ROME", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the Colosseum or St Peter's dome, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

Make the Colosseum arches the strongest shape; the dome and umbrella pines should complete the Roman read.
