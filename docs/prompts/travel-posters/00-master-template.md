# Travel Posters Master Template

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - [CITY NAME]

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for [City Name].

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep the destination 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "[City Name]" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: [ONE OR TWO LANDMARKS, named and described by their distinctive shape]
Secondary elements: [THREE TO FIVE SUPPORTING ELEMENTS, such as rooftops, river, bridges, hills, trees, street pattern, boats, towers, local architecture]

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the landmarks into generic circles, rectangles, or decorative symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "[CITY NAME]" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the primary landmark as the first focal point and the secondary elements arranged naturally around it.

Geometric language: use circles, arches, semicircles, stepped blocks, simplified rooftops, flat water bands, road ribbons, hillside shapes, and layered color fields to organize the scene. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmark.

Palette: [PALETTE LINE - name the light, season, or atmosphere, then list six to eight muted colors]. Use a limited sophisticated palette of 6-10 colors with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling. Avoid oversaturated primary colors.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "[CITY NAME]", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the landmark, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

Fill only the three creative slots: primary landmarks, secondary elements, and palette. The most important habit is to describe landmarks by shape, not just by name.
