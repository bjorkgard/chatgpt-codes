# London

![London travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/06-london.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - LONDON

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for London.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep London 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "London" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: Elizabeth Tower with a clear clock face and pointed gothic spire, and Tower Bridge with twin gothic towers and suspended bridge spans.
Secondary elements: the dome of St Paul's Cathedral, the Thames as a muted river band, brick terraces with chimney pots, a small red double-decker bus, plane trees.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the landmarks into generic circles, rectangles, or decorative symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "LONDON" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with Elizabeth Tower and Tower Bridge as the unmistakable anchors.

Geometric language: use bridge arches, clock circle, chimney rectangles, river bands, terrace blocks, dome arcs, and small red transit accents. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmark.

Palette: overcast London light with soft grey-blue, warm portland stone, muted brick red, deep green, pale cream, dusty slate, charcoal, and river blue. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "LONDON", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the clock tower or bridge towers, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

The clock face and Tower Bridge towers should be readable even when the image is small.
