# San Francisco

![San Francisco travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/12-san-francisco.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SAN FRANCISCO

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for San Francisco.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep San Francisco 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "San Francisco" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: the Golden Gate Bridge with tall vermilion towers and suspension cables, and the Transamerica Pyramid as a sharp triangular skyscraper.
Secondary elements: steep hills, rows of narrow painted Victorian houses, low fog rolling across the bay, cable car tracks running downhill, cypress trees, blue bay water.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the bridge towers or pyramid skyline into generic geometry.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "SAN FRANCISCO" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the bridge crossing the bay and steep urban layers behind it.

Geometric language: use bridge lines, cable arcs, hill diagonals, fog bands, painted-house blocks, bay strips, and cypress shapes. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmarks.

Palette: fog and late light with soft grey-white, muted vermilion, pale mint, dusty rose, cool bay blue, warm cream, cypress green, and charcoal. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "SAN FRANCISCO", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the Golden Gate Bridge, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

The bridge should stay readable through the fog; use fog as atmosphere, not as a cover for weak landmark shape.
