# Amsterdam

![Amsterdam travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/09-amsterdam.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - AMSTERDAM

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for Amsterdam.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep Amsterdam 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "Amsterdam" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: a row of tall narrow canal houses with stepped and bell-shaped gables, slightly varied rooflines, and slim vertical facades.
Secondary elements: arched canal bridges, facades leaning slightly forward, moored houseboats, elm trees along the water, bicycles near a railing, calm canal reflections.

Preserve the main architectural silhouettes, scale, proportions, and defining details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the canal houses into generic rectangles without their gabled identity.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "AMSTERDAM" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the canal-house row as the main focal shape.

Geometric language: use gable steps, narrow facade blocks, canal bands, arch bridges, bicycle circles, tree dots, and reflection stripes. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmark architecture.

Palette: soft northern daylight with muted brick red, deep canal green, warm cream, slate blue, dusty olive, pale grey, black-brown, and off-white. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "AMSTERDAM", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding the stepped and bell gables, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

Amsterdam works through a repeated architectural rhythm rather than a single monument; preserve the gable variety and canal line.
