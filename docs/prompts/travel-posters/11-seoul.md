# Seoul

![Seoul travel poster example](../../../examples/prompts/travel-posters/11-seoul.png)

## Prompt

```text
/travelposters
DESTINATION LANDMARK-LED GEOMETRIC ART POSTER - SEOUL

Create a sophisticated vertical travel art poster for Seoul.

Format: vertical poster, aspect ratio 2:3 or 3:4, high-resolution editorial travel print, generous upper negative space, calm stable composition, complete museum-shop artwork.

Core principle: keep Seoul 70% recognizable and 30% geometrically abstract. First depict the real landmark silhouettes and city identity clearly; then simplify the surrounding scene into refined geometric color blocks. The viewer should think "Seoul" within one second.

Landmark priority:
Primary landmarks: N Seoul Tower standing on the wooded slope of Namsan, and Gyeongbokgung Palace with curved tiled rooflines and traditional courtyard walls.
Secondary elements: the Han River with long low bridges, glass tower clusters, granite ridgelines behind the city, pine trees, palace courtyard paving, autumn foliage.

Preserve the main landmarks' recognizable shape, scale, proportions, and defining architectural details. Simplify only unnecessary detail. Do not reduce the tower or palace roofs into generic symbols.

Composition: keep the upper 40-50% as quiet sky or open negative space. Place only the destination name "SEOUL" at the upper center, small uppercase thin sans-serif lettering with wide spacing. Build the cityscape in the lower half with the palace roofs in front and N Seoul Tower rising above Namsan.

Geometric language: use roof curves, mountain layers, river bands, bridge lines, tower verticals, pine shapes, and modern building blocks. Geometry supports the city; it must never dominate the landmarks.

Palette: clear autumn light with pale sky blue, muted teal, warm granite grey, soft cream, deep pine green, dusty coral, brick red, and off-white. Use a limited sophisticated palette with a museum-print, gouache, and silkscreen feeling.

Texture and style: subtle handmade paper fibre, dry brush, gouache, colored pencil, slight pigment variation, clean contemporary fine-art print. Scandinavian editorial illustration, mid-century geometric art, landmark-led architectural poster, restrained color blocking.

Final test: recognizable at thumbnail size, elegant up close, poetic but specific, airy, architectural, collectible.

Constraints: exact spelling "SEOUL", no text other than the city name, no slogan, no subtitle, no over-abstraction, no dominant giant circles, no hiding N Seoul Tower or the palace roofs, no photorealism, no cartoon, no 3D rendering, no neon, no gold decoration, no sepia, no aged yellow paper, no watermark, no logos.
```

## Usage Notes

Balance old and new Seoul: palace roof curves in the foreground, tower and modern city rising behind.
