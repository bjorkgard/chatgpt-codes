# 1936 - Screen Siren

![1936 - Screen Siren example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/085-fashion-makeup-1936-screen-siren.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear reference selfie. Transform only the subject’s hair and makeup to create
an authentic beauty look representative of 1936. Preserve the subject’s recognizable
identity, facial features, face shape, skin tone, eye color, expression, pose, clothing,
jewelry, body, background, composition, and lighting. Do not alter the outfit, accessories,
or setting.

Create a smooth, softly powdered matte complexion with delicate rosy blush. Shape the
brows into very slender, elongated, dramatically arched curves characteristic of the era.
Add softly shaded neutral or smoky eyeshadow, subtle dark definition along the lash
lines, and delicately emphasized lashes. Give the lips a polished, clearly defined shape
with a pronounced cupid’s bow and rich rose, crimson, or berry-red color. Avoid modern
contouring, highlighting, glossy finishes, thick brows, or contemporary dramatic lashes.

Preserve the subject’s natural hair color and approximate length. Style the hair with a
deep side part and smooth, glossy sculpted waves that sweep closely around the
forehead and temples before transitioning into soft, polished curls. Arrange longer hair
neatly toward the back while maintaining the illusion of a sophisticated shorter style.

The finished image should look unmistakably 1936 through hair and makeup alone.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
