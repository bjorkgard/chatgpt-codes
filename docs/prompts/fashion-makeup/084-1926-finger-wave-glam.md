# 1926 - Finger Wave Glam

![1926 - Finger Wave Glam example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/084-fashion-makeup-1926-finger-wave-glam.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear reference selfie. Transform only the subject’s hair and makeup to create
an authentic beauty look representative of 1926. Preserve the subject’s recognizable
identity, facial features, face shape, skin tone, eye color, expression, pose, clothing,
jewelry, body, background, composition, and lighting. Do not alter the outfit, accessories,
or setting.

Create a smooth, softly powdered matte complexion with noticeable rosy rouge
concentrated high on the apples of the cheeks. Shape the brows into slender, gently
elongated arches while keeping them believable on the subject. Add smoky charcoal or
brown shadow around the eyes, emphasizing a softly rounded shape, with darkened
upper and lower lash lines and defined lashes. Give the lips a distinctly sculpted shape
with an emphasized cupid’s bow, slightly minimized outer corners, and a deep muted
crimson, berry, or wine tint. Avoid modern contouring, highlighting, glossy finishes, or
dramatic contemporary lashes.

Preserve the subject’s natural hair color and approximate length. Create sleek,
close-to-the-head styling with a deep side part and precise sculpted finger waves
framing the face, gathering longer hair discreetly at the back if necessary.

The finished image should look unmistakably 1926 through hair and makeup alone.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
