# Smokey Eye

![Smokey Eye example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/078-fashion-makeup-smokey-eye.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear, front-facing selfie in natural lighting. Preserve the subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, face
shape, skin tone, hairstyle length, eye color, expression, and overall likeness while applying a glamorous
1990s-inspired supermodel beauty transformation. Create a flawless soft-matte complexion with smooth, realistic skin
that retains natural texture while subtly minimizing redness, discoloration, blemishes, fine lines, and under-eye
darkness without appearing overly filtered or artificial. Sculpt the face with softly blended cool-toned contour beneath
the cheekbones, along the jawline, temples, and sides of the nose to create the strong, defined bone structure
associated with iconic runway beauty. Add a subtle satin highlight to the high points of the face rather than an
intensely dewy finish. Shape the brows into full, softly feathered brows with a gentle arch, maintaining a natural yet
polished appearance.

Make the eyes the unmistakable focal point with an iconic 90s supermodel smoky eye using layered matte charcoal,
espresso, taupe, and deep black shadows blended seamlessly from the lash line through the crease with beautifully
diffused edges. Intensify the outer corners into a lifted almond shape with elongated winged shadow that extends
outward toward the temples. Define both the upper and lower lash lines with rich black liner, softly smoked for a sultry
effect rather than a harsh graphic line. Tightline the upper waterline and softly smudge liner beneath the lower lashes
for added depth. Apply voluminous lengthening mascara and natural-looking wispy lashes that enhance the eyes
without appearing overly dramatic. Keep the lips softly sculpted with a matte nude rose, beige, or taupe lipstick paired
with subtle lip liner for the signature understated 90s supermodel finish.

Complete the transformation with sleek, glossy dark brunette hair styled in a timeless center part. Blow the hair
smooth with a silky, polished finish, tucked neatly behind the ears to showcase the sculpted face and dramatic eye
makeup. The hair should fall straight with soft movement and healthy shine, creating the effortless, luxurious runway
aesthetic of classic 1990s fashion editorials. Finish the portrait with soft studio lighting, a clean neutral background,
minimal gold hoop earrings, and an elegant editorial composition that feels sophisticated, high-fashion, timeless, and
photorealistic while preserving the subject’s authentic identity.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
