# Spa Day Skin (Dewy)

![Spa Day Skin (Dewy) example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/079-fashion-makeup-spa-day-skin-dewy.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear, front-facing selfie in natural lighting. Preserve the subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, face
shape, skin tone, hairstyle length, eye color, expression, and overall likeness while applying an ultra-luminous
spa-inspired “glass skin” beauty transformation. Create an exceptionally hydrated, healthy-looking complexion with
translucent, crystal-clear radiance that appears naturally lit from within rather than oily or overly filtered. Maintain
realistic skin texture with visible pores and subtle natural dimension while softly minimizing redness, uneven tone,
blemishes, fine lines, and under-eye darkness. Apply sheer, skin-like coverage that enhances the complexion without
masking it, allowing natural freckles and authentic skin character to remain visible where appropriate. Add delicate
luminosity across the forehead, temples, cheeks, bridge of the nose, cupid’s bow, chin, and collarbones to create a
fresh post-facial glow with soft reflective highlights. Gently sculpt the face using subtle cream contour beneath the
cheekbones, jawline, temples, and nose, blended seamlessly for barely-there definition. Apply a soft peachy-rose
cream blush high on the cheeks and lightly across the bridge of the nose for a healthy, youthful flush. Highlight the
eyes with champagne-toned glossy lids featuring a whisper of fine shimmer, softly defined lashes with lengthening
mascara, invisible tightlining, and brightened inner corners to create an awake, refreshed appearance without
dramatic eye makeup. Groom the brows into naturally full, fluffy, softly feathered brows with lifted hairs and clean
definition while preserving their original shape. Give the lips a smooth, hydrated appearance using a soft nude-pink
glossy balm with subtle volume, natural definition, and a glass-like finish. Style the hair into a sleek, center-parted,
ultra-glossy blowout with silky straight lengths, soft face-framing pieces, healthy shine, and neatly tucked sections
behind the ears for a luxurious minimalist aesthetic that complements the radiant skin. Use soft diffused daylight with
gentle frontal fill lighting to create realistic reflections across the skin without harsh shadows. The final result should
resemble a premium editorial beauty campaign immediately after a luxury facial treatment—fresh, refined, luminous,
effortlessly elegant, and photorealistic, with glowing glass skin as the unmistakable focal point.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
