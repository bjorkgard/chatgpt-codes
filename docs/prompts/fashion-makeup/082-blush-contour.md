# Blush Contour

![Blush Contour example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/082-fashion-makeup-blush-contour.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear, front-facing selfie in natural lighting. Preserve the subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, face
shape, skin tone, hairstyle length, eye color, expression, and overall likeness while applying an elegant soft gradient
blush contour makeup transformation inspired by modern editorial beauty. Create a flawless natural-satin complexion
with realistic skin texture that retains visible pores and authentic dimension while subtly minimizing redness, uneven
tone, blemishes, fine lines, and under-eye darkness using lightweight, skin-like coverage that enhances rather than
conceals. Softly sculpt the face using cool-neutral matte contour placed beneath the cheekbones, along the jawline,
temples, forehead, and sides of the nose, blending seamlessly so the definition appears naturally lifted rather than
sharply carved. Build the blush as the star of the look using a smooth gradient technique: begin with a muted rosy
nude close to the center of the cheeks, transitioning into soft peach-rose, then diffusing into a delicate warm pink as it
sweeps upward toward the temples. Continue a whisper of color across the bridge of the nose and lightly onto the
outer eye area so the blush and contour melt together into one continuous, airbrushed gradient with no visible edges.
Add a restrained satin highlight to the tops of the cheekbones, bridge of the nose, cupid’s bow, and inner corners for
a subtle glow rather than an intensely dewy finish. Keep the brows naturally full, softly groomed, and feathered with
gentle definition. Enhance the eyes with matte taupe, rosy beige, and soft cocoa shadows blended into a seamless
gradient, paired with softly lifted outer corners, tightlined upper lashes, wispy lengthening mascara, and a delicate
champagne inner-corner highlight. Finish the lips with softly blurred rosy-beige liner blended into the natural lip shape
and a satin nude rose lipstick or sheer rosy balm that complements the blush without overpowering it. Style the hair in
a sleek center part with softly defined natural waves or polished curls tucked gently behind the ears, creating a clean,
balanced frame that highlights the lifted blush placement and sculpted cheeks. Use soft window light or diffused
daylight with gentle front-facing illumination to emphasize the seamless blending and natural skin texture. The final
image should feel refined, expensive, softly romantic, and effortlessly beautiful, with the perfectly blended gradient
blush and subtle contour serving as the unmistakable focal points.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
