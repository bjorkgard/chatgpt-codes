# 2006 - Frosted Gloss

![2006 - Frosted Gloss example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/092-fashion-makeup-2006-frosted-gloss.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear reference selfie. Transform only the subject’s hair and makeup to create
an authentic beauty look representative of 2006. Preserve the subject’s recognizable
identity, facial features, face shape, skin tone, eye color, expression, pose, clothing,
jewelry, body, background, composition, and lighting. Do not alter the outfit, accessories,
or setting.

Create a smooth complexion with a softly matte finish and warm peachy or rosy blush.
Shape the brows into slim, neatly groomed arches. Add shimmery neutral eyeshadow in
champagne, bronze, taupe, or soft silver, with dark definition along the upper and lower
lash lines and noticeably emphasized lashes. Give the lips a glossy finish in pale pink,
peachy nude, or soft beige, with subtle liner if needed. Avoid modern fluffy brows, heavy
contouring, intense highlighting, overlined lips, or oversized lashes.

Preserve the subject’s natural hair color and approximate length. Create smooth, glossy
hair with a deep or slightly off-center side part, long face-framing layers, and sleek
straight lengths or softly flipped ends. Add gentle crown volume and a polished finish.

The finished image should look unmistakably 2006 through hair and makeup alone.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
