# 1916 - Powder & Pin Curls

![1916 - Powder & Pin Curls example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/083-fashion-makeup-1916-powder-pin-curls.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear reference selfie. Transform only the subject’s hair and makeup to create
an authentic beauty look representative of 1916. Preserve the subject’s recognizable
identity, facial features, face shape, skin tone, eye color, expression, pose, clothing,
jewelry, body, background, composition, and lighting. Do not alter the outfit, accessories,
or setting.

Create a softly powdered complexion with a natural matte finish and delicate rosy color
concentrated on the apples of the cheeks. Keep the brows natural, softly groomed, and
slightly defined. Add subtle dark definition close to the upper lash line and gently
deepen the lashes without creating an obvious modern eyeliner effect. Give the lips a
softly defined shape with a muted rose or berry tint and natural finish. Avoid contouring,
highlighting, glossy lips, dramatic lashes, or modern sculpted makeup.

Preserve the subject’s natural hair color and approximate length. Style the hair with soft
fullness around the sides and crown, a gentle off-center or center part, loose waves
around the face, and the remaining hair gracefully pinned into a low, softly structured
updo.

The finished image should look unmistakably 1916 through hair and makeup alone.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
