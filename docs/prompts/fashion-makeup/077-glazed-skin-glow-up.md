# Glazed Skin Glow-Up

![Glazed Skin Glow-Up example](../../../examples/prompts/fashion-makeup/077-fashion-makeup-glazed-skin-glow-up.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear, front-facing selfie in natural lighting. Preserve the subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, face
shape, skin tone, hairstyle length, eye color, expression, and overall likeness while applying a luxurious glazed-donut
skin and glossy lip beauty transformation. Create an ultra-hydrated, luminous complexion with radiant, light-reflecting
skin that appears naturally healthy rather than oily or overly filtered. Maintain realistic skin texture with visible pores
and authentic dimension while softly minimizing redness, uneven tone, blemishes, fine lines, and under-eye
darkness. Apply sheer, skin-like coverage that enhances rather than conceals, allowing natural freckles and skin
character to remain visible where appropriate. Add a soft pearlescent glow across the forehead, temples, cheeks,
bridge of the nose, cupid’s bow, chin, and collarbones to create the signature glazed finish. Sculpt the face with subtle
cream contour beneath the cheekbones, jawline, temples, and nose, blended seamlessly for effortless definition.
Apply a delicate neutral cream blush high on the cheeks with a hint across the bridge of the nose for a fresh, youthful
flush. Groom the brows into full, softly brushed-up, naturally fluffy brows with a clean, feathered shape. Keep the eyes
fresh and minimal with champagne-pearl cream shadow, softly defined lashes, subtle tightlining, and a touch of
inner-corner highlight that brightens without creating a dramatic makeup look. Give the lips a naturally fuller
appearance using a softly defined neutral rosy-beige liner blended into the lips, topped with a high-shine translucent
pink-nude gloss that creates a plump, juicy, mirror-like finish without visible glitter. Style the hair in a sleek,
center-parted blowout with silky, healthy-looking strands, subtle face-framing layers, and softly tucked sections
behind the ears to showcase the glowing complexion while maintaining an effortless, modern luxury aesthetic. Use
soft natural daylight with gentle front-facing illumination that enhances the reflective glow without blowing out
highlights. The final result should feel clean, expensive, editorial, and effortlessly beautiful, with radiant glazed skin
and glossy lips serving as the unmistakable focal points.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear front-facing selfie in natural light. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
