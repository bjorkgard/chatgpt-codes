# Giant Billboard Fashion

![Giant billboard fashion example](../../examples/prompts/05-giant-billboard-fashion.png)

## Prompt

```text
Use the attached image as the primary visual reference. Preserve the woman's visible physical characteristics as faithfully as possible: same facial structure and proportions, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, approximate age, long straight highlighted brunette hair with center part, natural expression, and overall appearance.

Create an ultra-photorealistic luxury fashion advertising scene in a busy modern downtown business district at golden hour. The woman appears monumentally oversized, realistically integrated into the city architecture, sitting elegantly on the lower frame of a gigantic illuminated billboard attached to a glass-and-steel skyscraper.

She wears a sophisticated beige tailored pantsuit with an oversized blazer and wide-leg trousers, a fitted black top, and elegant pointed black high heels. One knee is raised onto the billboard ledge, elbow resting casually on it, hand near her hair; the other leg hangs naturally toward the street. Relaxed posture, subtle confident closed-mouth smile, direct warm eye contact.

Billboard: warm ivory background, thick matte-black frame. Large elegant black serif typography on the left: "Own Your Every Step." Smaller text underneath: "Confident looks good on you."

Below her, create a highly detailed metropolitan intersection with pedestrians crossing, city buses, cars, storefronts, sidewalks and realistic reflections across glass facades, emphasizing her enormous scale. Warm low-angle sunlight illuminates her face and hair with delicate golden rim light, soft realistic shadows and cinematic dynamic range.

Extremely realistic human skin: visible pores, subtle fine lines, natural tonal variation, tiny imperfections, realistic subsurface scattering, absolutely no plastic or artificial skin. Individual hair strands, flyaways, realistic fabric weave, stitching, folds and reflections on leather heels. Premium editorial fashion campaign aesthetic.

Shot on Sony A1, 50mm f/1.8 lens, architectural perspective, deep environmental detail, natural depth of field, HDR, razor-sharp 8K, photorealistic, cinematic color grading, physically accurate lighting, realistic scale and shadows.
```

## Usage Notes

This is an identity-preserving fashion transformation prompt. If using a real reference image, keep the preservation instructions near the top and separate them from the scene description.
