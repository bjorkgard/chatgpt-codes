# Modern Renaissance Portrait

![Modern Renaissance Portrait example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/052-selfie-transformation-modern-renaissance-portrait.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload one clear portrait. Use it to faithfully preserve the subject's recognizable identity, including facial structure,
proportions, skin tone, eye shape, hairstyle, age, expression, and overall likeness. Transform them into a
museum-quality oil portrait inspired by European Renaissance painting (c. 1450–1500), depicting the same person in
contemporary tailoring.

Pose the subject in a classic three-quarter composition with an upright posture, calm expression, and soft, confident
gaze. One hand should rest naturally on a table or armrest while the other relaxes in the lap, emphasizing graceful
Renaissance-inspired hand placement.

Dress the subject in a perfectly tailored charcoal or black suit jacket with a crisp white open-collar shirt. Exclude ties,
logos, patterns, or visible branding.

Illuminate the portrait with soft window light from one side, creating gentle chiaroscuro and natural, painterly
transitions. Use a simple hand-painted plaster background in warm earth tones such as umber, sienna, olive, gray,
and muted ochre.

Render the painting with layered oil glazes, subtle sfumato, authentic brushwork, fine canvas texture, and delicate
craquelure. Preserve natural skin texture while avoiding HDR, glossy finishes, excessive sharpness, cinematic
effects, or modern photographic styling. The finished artwork should feel timeless, refined, and worthy of a
Renaissance palace or museum.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
