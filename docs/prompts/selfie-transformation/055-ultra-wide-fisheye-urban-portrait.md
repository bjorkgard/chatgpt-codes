# Ultra-Wide Fisheye Urban Portrait

![Ultra-Wide Fisheye Urban Portrait example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/055-selfie-transformation-ultra-wide-fisheye-urban-portrait.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload one clear selfie. Preserve the subject's recognizable identity, facial features, hairstyle, expression, skin tone,
body proportions, and overall likeness. Reimagine them as though photographed with an extreme circular fisheye
lens (8–10mm equivalent) from a very low angle, creating dramatic perspective with slightly enlarged feet, elongated
legs, and skyscrapers curving inward around the subject.

Place the subject confidently in the center of a modern financial district surrounded by sleek glass towers. Frame the
entire scene within a perfect circular fisheye image with natural black vignetting. Position the camera just above
ground level looking upward so the architecture rises dramatically while remaining centered behind the subject.

Dress the subject in timeless, unbranded streetwear including oversized pleated trousers, clean white sneakers, a
fitted white shirt, and a lightweight zip-up jacket with minimal detailing. Preserve the hairstyle and use natural makeup
with a polished everyday look.

Pose with relaxed shoulders, feet shoulder-width apart, and hands naturally in pockets or at the sides. Illuminate the
scene with soft daylight beneath a lightly overcast sky, revealing realistic reflections, crisp architectural details,
balanced exposure, and subtle pavement textures. The finished image should feel bold, cinematic, immersive, and
editorial while keeping the subject instantly recognizable.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
