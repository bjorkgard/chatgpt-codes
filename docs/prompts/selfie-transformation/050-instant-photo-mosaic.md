# Instant Photo Mosaic

![Instant Photo Mosaic example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/050-selfie-transformation-instant-photo-mosaic.png)

## Prompt

```text
Transform the subject into a striking editorial portrait constructed entirely from overlapping instant-photo prints.
Preserve the subject’s recognizable facial features, skin tone, age range, hair color, hairstyle, and overall likeness.

Place the subject centered against a dark charcoal, subtly textured tabletop or backdrop. Reconstruct her portrait
using approximately 18–24 individual white-bordered photographs arranged vertically in an imperfect collage. Each
print should reveal a different cropped section of the same portrait—eyes, lips, hair, shoulders, clothing, arms, and
torso—aligning together like a fragmented photographic puzzle.

Vary the photographs in size, angle, position, and overlap while maintaining enough alignment for the complete
person to remain immediately recognizable and wholly “put together” without major gaps in the resemblance. Allow

white borders to cross naturally over facial features, hair, and clothing, creating dimensional layers and realistic paper
shadows.

Include two realistic hands entering from opposite edges, actively placing or adjusting individual photographs,
suggesting the collage is being assembled in real time.

Use soft directional studio lighting, realistic paper texture, subtle shadows, crisp photographic detail, natural skin
texture, and slightly imperfect placement. Keep the palette neutral and sophisticated. Compose vertically with the
fragmented portrait filling most of the frame.

4:5 aspect ratio
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.
