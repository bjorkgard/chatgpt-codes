# Immersive Beverage POV

![Immersive Beverage POV example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/054-selfie-transformation-immersive-beverage-pov.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a reference photo. Use it as inspiration for the subject's recognizable identity, facial features, hairstyle,
expression, and overall presence while freely reimagining the wardrobe, pose, lighting, composition, camera angle,

background, colors, and artistic interpretation. Prioritize the immersive concept over faithfully recreating the original
image.

Position the camera inside a large transparent glass, looking upward through the circular opening. The subject leans
over the glass, makes eye contact with the viewer, and sips through a colorful bendable straw that extends toward the
camera, creating dramatic depth and perspective.

Fill the glass with crushed ice, frozen slush, juicy fruit pieces, sparkling bubbles, condensation, and melting ice
crystals that naturally frame the lens, creating the illusion that the viewer is inside a refreshing drink.

Use an ultra-wide or fisheye perspective with subtle edge distortion while keeping facial proportions realistic. Dress
the subject in soft casual clothing with fresh pastel colors and natural textures. Set the scene outdoors beneath a
bright blue sky with crisp sunlight that creates sparkling reflections and realistic skin tones.

The finished image should feel whimsical, vibrant, photorealistic, and highly immersive, combining dramatic
wide-angle photography with refreshing summer textures and a polished editorial portrait aesthetic.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
