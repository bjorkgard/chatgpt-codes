# Instant Photo Birthday

![Instant Photo Birthday example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/051-selfie-transformation-instant-photo-birthday.png)

## Prompt

```text
Ultra-realistic lifestyle birthday photograph during a dim, intimate evening celebration, featuring a playful
photo-within-a-photo composition. In the extreme foreground, a feminine hand with French-tip nails, stacked silver
rings, and natural skin texture holds a chunky butter-yellow instant camera, occupying roughly the lower third of the
frame. A white-bordered photograph emerges vertically from the camera as the main focal point.

Inside the photograph, preserve the reference subject’s recognizable identity, facial features, skin tone, and hair color.
Show her in elegant side profile against a pale studio wall, holding a small yellow birthday cake with number candles

displaying [insert age]. Dress her in a sophisticated sleeved black evening dress with a sparkling choker and
understated makeup. Style her hair into a sleek low ponytail with a center part and natural flyaways. She gently
purses her lips toward the candles while direct flash creates a crisp silhouette behind her.

Outside the photograph, place a larger vintage-inspired yellow cake on a dark wooden table with delicate piping and
a glowing candle. Add softly blurred greenery, cream and blush flowers, and metallic balloons. Use direct flash, warm
candlelight, shallow depth of field, realistic shadows, subtle grain, natural skin texture, and imperfect details for an
authentic spontaneous snapshot.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
