# Photobooth Expression Grid

![Photobooth Expression Grid example](../../../examples/prompts/selfie-transformation/057-selfie-transformation-photobooth-expression-grid.png)

## Prompt

```text
Upload a clear selfie reference photo. Transform the subject into a timeless black-and-white photobooth expression
grid, using the reference primarily as inspiration for the subject’s identity, facial features, hairstyle, expression style,

and overall presence rather than something to recreate exactly. Lean heavily into the classic photobooth aesthetic
while allowing the wardrobe, pose sequence, lighting, composition, and artistic interpretation to change as needed.
Prioritize creating a believable vintage photobooth contact sheet over preserving the original photograph.

Reimagine the subject wearing a simple solid black crew-neck top with no visible logos, patterns, jewelry, hats, or
distracting accessories. Style the hair naturally with minimal movement so the facial expressions remain the focus.
Use soft, even studio lighting against a seamless light gray or off-white backdrop with subtle tonal variation. Render
the entire image in rich monochrome with crisp detail, soft contrast, and a clean editorial finish reminiscent of
traditional silver gelatin portrait photography.

Arrange the final composition as a perfectly aligned 4×4 grid consisting of 16 equally sized square portraits separated
by thin white borders and surrounded by a clean white outer margin. Keep the framing identical in every square—a
straight-on head-and-shoulders portrait with the subject centered, maintaining consistent camera distance, crop,
lighting, and background throughout. The only variation should be the subject’s expressions and hand gestures.

Populate the grid with a diverse range of authentic, playful expressions such as smiling, laughing, surprised,
confused, skeptical, pouting, winking, sticking out the tongue, puffed cheeks, exaggerated frowns, raised eyebrows,
mock frustration, sleepy, excited, thoughtful, and mischievous looks. Include occasional expressive hand gestures
near the face—such as peace signs, finger frames, cheek rests, playful “horns,” open hands, or mock claws—but
keep every pose feeling candid, natural, and spontaneous rather than theatrical. Ensure each square captures a
distinctly different emotion so the full grid feels energetic, charming, and full of personality while maintaining the
polished consistency of a professional photobooth contact sheet.
```

## Usage Notes

Use these with a clear selfie or portrait reference. The example images use a fictional subject where the original prompt expects an uploaded photo.

The example image shown for this file uses made-up input details so the prompt can be previewed without a real upload.
