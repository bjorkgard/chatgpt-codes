# Phone Coffee Stream

![Phone coffee stream example](../../examples/prompts/02-phone-coffee-stream.png)

## Prompt

```text
A hand holds a smartphone, and on the screen is a man with a friendly smile. The man in the screen is pouring hot coffee from a gold metal kettle.

What makes this image unique and surreal is that the coffee stream appears to be flowing from the phone screen and falling into a real white coffee cup, which sits on a saucer on a wooden table. Steam can be seen rising from the cup, indicating the coffee is still hot.
```

## Usage Notes

This prompt is strongest when the image clearly connects the digital world to the physical world through one continuous coffee stream. Keep the screen, kettle, cup, and steam readable.
